Dreams of Mother - Episode Three

[pause-3]

Winston was dreaming of his mother and his younger sister. He must have been ten years old in the dream. 

[pause]

They were both sinking into darkness, far below him, their faces turned upward, reaching desperately toward him. Yet they kept sinking deeper and deeper, while he remained motionless, floating in lighter air.

[pause-2]

In the dream, as in all his dreams of his mother, she appeared as he remembered her from his last glimpse of her. She was a tall, statuesque woman with magnificent fair hair, wearing a thin dress that showed the outline of her noble figure.

[pause]

He remembered especially the delicate thinness of her shoes, and how they seemed to emphasize the grace of her movements.

[pause-2]

But both she and his sister were sinking through the dark water, down, down, down... all because of his presence above them. They were sinking to their deaths so that he could live.

[pause]

Winston woke with the word "Mother!" on his lips.

[pause-3]

His heart was pounding violently. Tears stung his eyes. 

[pause]

He had been dreaming of his mother, and sometime in the thirty years since he had last seen her, he had forgotten what she looked like. Tomorrow he would dream of her again, and in the dream she would not sink, but stand in the bright sunlight, a warm smile on her face.

[pause-2]

The dream was still vivid in his mind as he mechanically walked the dreary corridors of the Ministry of Truth.

[pause]

But dreams, he knew, were dangerous things to remember.

[pause-3]
