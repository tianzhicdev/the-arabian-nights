The Diary - Episode Two

[pause-3]

Winston's heart nearly stopped as he reached for the door handle. The diary... he had left it open on the table.

[pause-2]

DOWN WITH BIG BROTHER - the words stared back at him from the cream-colored pages, written over and over in his desperate handwriting.

[pause]

He crossed the room in three quick strides and slammed the book shut. But the damage was already done. The ink had surely soaked through. Even closed, the diary would still carry those treasonous words.

[pause-2]

His hands trembled as he picked up the heavy volume. Should he destroy it now? Burn the pages? But that would be even more suspicious - the smell of burning paper might attract attention.

[pause]

No. The safest thing was to carry on as if nothing had happened. To betray no sign of worry or guilt.

[pause-2]

Winston carefully tucked the diary back into its hiding place. As he did so, he noticed something peculiar - a tiny speck of white dust on the cover.

[pause]

His blood ran cold.

[pause-2]

Someone had touched the diary while he was gone. The Thought Police? A spy? Or was his mind playing tricks on him?

[pause-3]

In Oceania, paranoia wasn't just a state of mind - it was a survival instinct.

[pause-2]

Winston forced himself to take slow, steady breaths. He had to maintain control. Had to keep his face blank, his movements natural. The telescreen was always watching.

[pause]

Always listening.

[pause-3]

But deep inside, where even the Party couldn't reach, his mind screamed a single thought:

[pause]

What have I done?
